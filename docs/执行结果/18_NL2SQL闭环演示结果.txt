问：销量最高的前 5 个品类？
SQL：SELECT p.category, SUM(i.quantity) AS total_qty FROM dwd_order_item i JOIN dim_product p ON i.product_id = p.product_id GROUP BY p.category ORDER BY total_qty DESC LIMIT 5
